How It Works

✍️

1. Write & Lock

Write your message and choose your unlock date. Add attachments, set a password, and personalize your time capsule.

🔐

2. Encrypt & Deploy

Your message is encrypted using AES-256-GCM and split into fragments distributed across multiple blockchains using Shamir Secret Sharing.

📬

3. Automatic Unlock

On your chosen date, the message fragments are automatically retrieved and reassembled. You'll receive an email notification.

🔒 Cryptographically Secure

No one can access your message before the unlock date—not even us. We use Shamir Secret Sharing (3-of-5 threshold) to ensure your message requires multiple blockchain fragments to decrypt.
